benchside

Browser-only tools for taking apart emails, ZIPs, PDFs and more. Your files never leave your machine.

  • Mail analyzer

    Checks SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and ARC for one message and shows the header fields each check read.

  • ZIP analyzer

    Lists entries, compares local and central headers, opens nested archives, and shows the bytes.

  • PDF analyzer

    Shows revisions, objects, pages, and every action and script, without running any of them.

  • DMARC aggregate report analyzer

    Reads a folder of rua reports and shows who sends mail as your domain and whether it is ready for reject.

Open source: github.com/photom/benchside
